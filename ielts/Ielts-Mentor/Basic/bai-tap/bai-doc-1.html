ĐỀ 01

Chủ đề: Nghề nghiệp • Thói quen hằng ngày • Chuyến đi

Reading Test 1

Passage 1

My uncle is a doctor. He works in a hospital in the city, and his job is very important. Every day, he helps sick people feel better. He checks their health, gives them medicine, and sometimes tells them to stay in the hospital for a few days. He usually works from Monday to Friday, but sometimes he also has to work on the weekend. His job is not easy because he meets many patients and must always stay calm and careful. He often works long hours, but he doesn’t complain because he loves his job. He says the best part of being a doctor is seeing his patients get well and smile again. When he comes home, he is sometimes very tired, but he still enjoys spending time with his family. I think his job is both hard and meaningful.

Passage 2

Andrew has a busy day. He usually wakes up at 6:30 in the morning. After that, he washes his face, brushes his teeth, and eats breakfast with his family. At 7:30, he goes to work by bus. He starts work at 8 o’clock in the office. In the morning, he answers emails and talks to his colleagues. At 12:30, Andrew has lunch at a small restaurant near his office. In the afternoon, he continues working on his projects. He finishes work at 5:30 and goes home. In the evening, Andrew likes to relax. Sometimes he watches TV, sometimes he reads a book, and sometimes he goes for a walk. At 7 o’clock, he has dinner with his family. After dinner, he spends time talking with his parents and younger sister. Before going to bed, Andrew checks his phone for a while. He usually goes to sleep at 10:30.

Passage 3

Maya went on a trip to the mountains last weekend. She went with her two best friends. They left their town early in the morning by bus and arrived at the mountains after three hours. The weather was cool and fresh, and the sky was very clear. Maya enjoyed walking on the small paths and looking at the tall green trees. She also took many photos of the beautiful views. At lunchtime, they had sandwiches and fruit that they brought from home. In the afternoon, Maya and her friends climbed a small hill. It was not easy, but they were very happy when they reached the top. From there, they could see the whole valley and a river below. They stayed until the evening and then returned home by bus. Maya was very tired, but she said it was one of the best trips she had ever had.

ĐỀ 02

Chủ đề: Cuối tuần • Trường học • Quần áo

Reading Test 2

Passage 1

Ken had a very nice weekend. On Saturday morning, he woke up early and went to the park near his house. He enjoyed walking and breathing the fresh air. After that, he met his friend Tom and they played football together for an hour. At lunchtime, Ken went home and ate noodles with his family. In the afternoon, he helped his mother clean the house and then watched a movie on TV.

On Sunday, Ken stayed at home in the morning. He read a comic book and listened to music. In the afternoon, his family went shopping at the supermarket. Ken bought some snacks and drinks. In the evening, they had a big dinner with rice, chicken, and vegetables. After dinner, Ken spent time talking with his parents. Finally, he went to bed early because he had school on Monday.

Ken felt happy and relaxed after the weekend.

Passage 2

Mark is a student at Green Hill School. He usually wakes up at 6:30 in the morning and gets ready for class. After having breakfast, he walks to school because it is not far from his house. His first lesson starts at 8 o’clock. Mark enjoys studying English and Math the most because he likes learning new words and solving problems.

During the break, Mark often plays games with his classmates in the schoolyard. Sometimes they play football, and sometimes they just talk and laugh together. At lunchtime, he eats rice and vegetables in the school canteen.

In the afternoon, Mark has science or history lessons. He listens to the teacher carefully and takes notes. After school, he goes home, does his homework, and then helps his mother with some housework. Mark feels happy at school because he can study and spend time with his friends every day.

Passage 3

Lily is a young student who loves wearing simple but nice clothes. On school days, she usually wears a white shirt and a blue skirt, because that is her school uniform. She also wears black shoes and white socks. Sometimes, she puts on a blue jacket when the weather is cool.

At home, Lily likes wearing T-shirts and jeans. She has many T-shirts in different colors, such as red, yellow, and green. Her favorite T-shirt is pink because it looks very cute. She usually wears her pink T-shirt with light blue jeans and comfortable sandals.

On weekends, Lily enjoys wearing dresses. She has a long purple dress for parties and a short yellow dress for summer days. She also has a hat that she wears when she goes out in the sun. Lily thinks her clothes are not only comfortable but also beautiful.

ĐỀ 03

Chủ đề: Thể thao • Hiệu sách • Robot

Reading Test 3

Passage 1

David is a young athlete who trains very hard every day. He loves running and wants to be one of the fastest runners in his city. Every morning, David wakes up early and goes jogging in the park. He usually runs for about one hour to improve his speed and health. After that, he does some stretching and simple exercises to keep his body strong.

At school, David also plays football with his classmates. He is very quick on the field, and his friends like having him on their team. On weekends, he joins a sports club where he learns new skills and practices with other athletes.

David feels happy and proud when he plays sports.

Passage 2

Uncle Tom has a small bookstore in the town center. The shop is not very big, but it has many interesting books. There are books for children, students, and adults. On the shelves, you can see storybooks, comic books, English books, and cookbooks. The bookstore is always clean, so people like to visit.

Every morning, Uncle Tom opens the store at 8 o’clock. He welcomes his customers with a smile. Many children come after school to read comics and fairy tales. Sometimes, Uncle Tom helps them choose books.

On weekends, the bookstore is busier. Families come to buy books, and parents often choose English books for their kids. Uncle Tom feels happy because his bookstore is a place for reading and learning.

Passage 3

Brian has two robots at his home, and they help his family every day. The first robot is a cleaning robot. It can sweep the floor, wash the dishes, and clean the windows. Brian’s mother likes this robot very much because it saves her a lot of time.

The second robot is a cooking robot. It can cook rice, make soup, and prepare breakfast for the family. Sometimes, it also makes sandwiches for Brian when he goes to school. The food is always fresh and tasty.

Brian’s robots are also smart. They can answer simple questions, tell the time, and play music. At night, the cooking robot can even tell a bedtime story to Brian and his little sister.

Brian thinks they make life easier, more comfortable, and more fun for the whole family.

ĐỀ 04

Chủ đề: Thời tiết • Ca sĩ • Phong cách Nhật Bản

Reading Test 4

Passage 1

Last summer, Lina went on holiday with her family. They traveled to a small beach town by the sea. The weather during her holiday was very nice. In the morning, the sky was blue, and the sun was bright. It was warm, so Lina and her brother played on the beach and swam in the sea.

In the afternoon, the weather was sometimes hot, so Lina wore a hat and sunglasses. She also drank a lot of water to stay cool. One day, there were some white clouds, but it did not rain. The wind was gentle, and it felt fresh near the sea.

In the evening, the weather became cooler. Lina and her family walked along the beach and enjoyed the sunset. She liked the cool air after a hot day. Lina thinks the weather on her holiday was perfect for relaxing and having fun.

Passage 2

Sơn Tùng M-TP is a very popular singer in Vietnam. He was born on July 5, 1994, in Thái Bình province. When he was a child, he loved music and enjoyed singing. Later, he studied at the Conservatory of Music in Ho Chi Minh City to improve his talent.

Sơn Tùng became famous in 2012 with his first songs. His music is modern, with catchy rhythms and meaningful lyrics. Some of his most famous songs are Em của ngày hôm qua, Chúng ta không thuộc về nhau, and Lạc trôi. Young people in Vietnam like his songs because they are easy to remember and fun to sing.

He is handsome, with short black hair and a bright smile. He often wears stylish clothes on stage, which makes him look confident and cool. Besides singing, Sơn Tùng also writes songs and performs in big concerts. He works very hard and always tries new styles in his music. Today, he is one of the most successful singers in Vietnam and a role model for many fans.

Passage 3

People in Japan often have a neat and clean appearance. Many men and women have straight black or dark brown hair. They usually take care of their skin and dress carefully. Japanese people like simple and comfortable clothes, but they also follow fashion trends.

In cities like Tokyo, young people wear modern and colorful outfits. Some like casual clothes such as jeans, T-shirts, and sneakers. Others enjoy stylish dresses or suits for special occasions. People often pay attention to shoes, bags, and accessories to look good.

Japanese people also value manners and neatness in their appearance. They often look tidy and polite in public. Their style shows respect for others and confidence. Many visitors admire Japanese people because of their clean, fashionable, and smart appearance.
